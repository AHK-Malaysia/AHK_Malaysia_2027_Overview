About AHK Malaysia

The bilateral bridge, since 1991

We are the official representative of German industry and trade in Malaysia, and the largest bilateral European chamber in the country.

We represent our members' interests with governments and authorities in both countries, and we connect them to business organisations and industry across Malaysia and Germany.

01Automotive

Electrification, local content requirements and investment incentives for OEMs and suppliers.

Milestone
Position paper on incentives submitted to the Ministry of Finance, jointly with the EU Automotive Group.
Called for the sales tax exemption on locally assembled (CKD) vehicles to be extended.
02Energy Transformation

Grid access, renewable energy supply and industrial input to the National Energy Transition Roadmap.

Milestone
White paper in preparation. Member of the Ministry of Economy advisory council on power and grid transformation.
03Talent

Skills development, TVET and the recruitment of qualified staff.

2027 focus
A skills-gap survey of members, leading to recommendations on TVET and dual vocational training for the Ministry of Human Resources.
04Critical Minerals

Rare earth processing, supply chain security and German technology for sustainable mining and refining.

2027 focus
Dialogue on the National Critical Minerals and Rare Earth strategy, and German-Malaysian partnerships in downstream processing.
